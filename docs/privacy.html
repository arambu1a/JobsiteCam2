FieldStamp Privacy Policy

Effective August 26, 2026

FieldStamp does not collect any of your data. The app has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. Everything it does happens on your iPhone.

What the app accesses, and why

Camera. FieldStamp uses the camera to take jobsite photos. Photos are captured and stamped with the overlay entirely on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.

Location (while using the app). If you grant location access, FieldStamp reads your current position once so it can print the street address onto the photo overlay. To turn coordinates into a readable address, the app uses Apple's built-in geocoding service, which means your approximate coordinates are sent to Apple for that lookup. This is handled by iOS under Apple's privacy policy; the developer of FieldStamp neither receives nor stores your location. If you deny location access, the app simply leaves the address line blank and everything else keeps working.

Photo library (saving only). FieldStamp requests add-only access so it can save stamped photos to your camera roll. It cannot read, browse, or view the photos already in your library.

What is stored on your device

Photos you save to a PM group, along with their PM number, date, and address, are written to FieldStamp's private storage area on your iPhone. This data is visible only to the app. Deleting FieldStamp removes all of it permanently.

Sharing

When you tap Share, FieldStamp hands the photo to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there — Messages, Mail, Files, or any other app — is entirely your choice, and is governed by whichever app you pick.

What FieldStamp does not do

  • No data is transmitted to the developer or to any third party
  • No accounts, logins, or personal details are requested
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone

Children

FieldStamp is a work tool intended for general audiences. It collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
