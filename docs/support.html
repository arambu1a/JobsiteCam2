What FieldStamp does

FieldStamp burns a timestamp overlay directly into your jobsite photos, so the date, address, and project details are part of the image itself rather than metadata that gets stripped when the photo is emailed or texted.

The overlay can include:

  • Date — filled in automatically
  • Location — your current street address, resolved automatically
  • PM # — the project or work-order number
  • Notification #
  • Foreman
  • Photo type — Progress, Before, After, and so on

Each field can be switched on or off in Settings, so the bar shows only what your crew actually needs.

Using the app

Taking a stamped photo

Point and shoot. The overlay preview sits over the viewfinder so you can see exactly what will be burned in. After the shot, you can edit any field before saving.

Saving

Save to Roll writes the stamped photo to your iPhone's camera roll, ready to share anywhere.

Save to PM files it into a folder for that PM number, kept inside the app. Enter a PM # in the overlay first — the app will prompt you if it's blank.

PM folders

Open the folders view to see every PM number you've photographed, with the most recent first. Tap into one for the photo grid, where you can select multiple photos to share or delete at once.

Common questions

The address says "Locating…" or "No location"

FieldStamp needs location permission to fill in the address. Go to Settings › FieldStamp › Location and choose While Using the App. "No location" means permission was denied; the rest of the app still works normally, just without the address line.

The address is wrong or imprecise

Addresses come from Apple's geocoding service. On large sites or in rural areas the nearest known address may be some distance off. You can tap the location field and type the correct address before saving.

Photos aren't saving to my camera roll

Check Settings › FieldStamp › Photos and make sure adding photos is allowed. FieldStamp only ever adds photos — it never reads your library.

Where do my PM photos live?

Inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them permanently, so save anything you need to keep to your camera roll or share it out first.

Can I get my photos off the phone?

Yes — open a PM folder, select the photos you want, and use Share to send them by email, message, or into Files.
